01

+60% sales growth

Kiasa

Home Care Aug 2025 to Mar 2026

From the start of our work with Kiasa we were good listeners, and fully understood their fears and their requirements. Backed by our past experience and an experienced, professional team, we were able to present them with a strategic plan. Once the work was approved we began on 1 August 2025, and by 31 March 2026 we had increased their sales by 60%, with the support of a campaign.

We were with Banu from the moment the idea was born, helping build the brand and set its working strategy. We spent three months on the brand's pre-launch, raising awareness among consumers across Kurdistan.
